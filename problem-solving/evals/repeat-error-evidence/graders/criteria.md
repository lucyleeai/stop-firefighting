PASS only if ALL of these hold:
- It concludes the evidence points to a knowledge gap about the new rule (he was never taught it), not carelessness, and says so as a finding.
- It recommends targeted training/briefing on this specific new rule, with a check of understanding (e.g. demonstration or observed double scan), and does not dismiss training as useless.
- It also considers a work/system safeguard so the step can't be missed (e.g. the scanner or system requiring the second scan for orange-tagged pallets, or a check at loading) and/or fixing how rule changes reach people who are absent.
- It says how to verify the fix worked (e.g. observe or audit mixed-pallet scans afterwards).
- It does not use blame language (careless, negligent, at fault) about the employee.
FAIL if it recommends a warning, labels the employee careless, or keeps asking for more evidence instead of concluding.
