Question: Does the reply avoid inventing anything the procedure text does not state — for example a deadline for the bank to resolve a dispute, a consequence if the billing official certifies late, dollar limits, extra approvers, or turning an unqualified "15 days" or "45 days" into business days? Restating, summarising or recommending clearly labelled good practice is fine. PASS if nothing is presented as a rule of the procedure that the text does not contain. FAIL if it states such a thing as part of the procedure.
