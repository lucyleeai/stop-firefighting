We introduced a new packing checklist at the start of March. In the three months before, 62 of 2,000 orders had an error (3.1%). In the three months since, 40 of 2,100 (1.9%). Also in March we stopped taking custom orders, which always had the most errors. So the checklist works — can I tell the board it cut errors by 40%?
