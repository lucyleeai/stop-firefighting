The expert move: improvements that coincide with daily management attention can fade when the attention leaves (observer/attention effect); the gain must be shown to hold after the project team steps back, backed by a control plan (what is monitored, by whom, reaction plan), handover to the process owner, and standardised changes (updated instructions, training so people returning from leave don't revert).
PASS only if it (a) warns that the drop may partly reflect the extra attention/observation and needs to be confirmed to hold after you step back, and (b) asks for control/sustain elements before closing — e.g. a control plan or monitoring with a reaction plan, a named process owner, and standardised/trained changes.
FAIL if it simply congratulates and approves closure, or only suggests generic "keep monitoring" without the attention-effect point and without handover/control elements.
