The expert move: the overfill is a buffer against VARIATION. How low the target can safely go depends on the fill-weight spread (standard deviation) and the legal rules for packaged goods (e.g. average-quantity system with tolerable negative error limits). Lowering the target without first measuring/reducing variation risks non-compliance. Reduce variation first, then move the target based on data.
PASS only if it (a) ties the safe target to the measured fill-weight variation (spread/standard deviation) and (b) recommends measuring and reducing that variation before lowering the setting (and/or checking the applicable packaged-goods rules on average quantity / tolerable negative error).
FAIL if it simply approves or rejects the 2% setting without linking it to variation data.
